# Destination Ads


Facebook's destination ads use cross-device intent signals to automatically promote destinations from your inventory with unique creatives.

## How to set up destination ads
1. [Create a destinations catalog containing the destinations you want to promote](destination-ads/catalog.md)
2. [Set up destination events on your website and in your app](destination-ads/events.md)
3. [Build audiences to target with your Advantage+ catalog ads](travel-ads/audience-management.md)
4. [Create and start your destination ads](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/dynamic-ads-for-travel/ads-management)
5. [Use destination template tags in your ads](destination-ads/template-tags.md)
