# Advantage+ Catalog Ads



With [Advantage+ catalog ads](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/2486309305148646), you can promote relevant items from an entire catalog across any device. Display ads for thousands of items to the right audience and automate the process. The solution has three major elements:

1. [Create a feed from your catalog](catalog.md) to automatically deliver images, descriptions, and prices in your ads.
2. Set up [App Events](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/app-events) or [Meta Pixel](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/meta-pixel) to measure actions, such as purchases, and profile target [audiences](audiences/guides/dynamic-product-audiences.md) for ads.
3. [Create and deliver ads based on your catalog](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/ads-commerce/marketing-api/dynamic-ads/get-started). The ads display relevant items from your catalog feed and target your audience.

Learn how to [get started with Advantage+ Catalog Ads](advantage-catalog-ads/get-started.md).
